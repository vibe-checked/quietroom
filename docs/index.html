Ambient noise for iPhone, generated on your device — no streaming, no ads between tracks, no account.

v1.0.0·iPhone · iOS 16+

01

Overview

How it works

Pick a sound. Tap the big play button. Set a sleep timer if you want it to fade out on its own.

  • White — even hiss across all frequencies. Cuts conversation cleanly.
  • Pink — softer, lower energy than white. Closer to leaves moving in wind.
  • Brown — deep, rumbling. Surf, distant waterfall.
  • Rain — filtered noise with scattered patter to suggest drops.
02

How the sound is made

Where do the sounds come from?

They're synthesized in JavaScript the first time you tap play. The app computes 10-second WAV files for each noise type using classic noise-generation algorithms (Paul Kellett's pink filter, integrated Brownian motion for brown noise, low-passed pink + scattered impulses for rain), writes them to the app's cache directory, then loops them through the audio engine.

This means: no audio files shipped with the app, no streaming, no licensed samples to worry about, and after first launch playback works fully offline.

03

Sleep timer

How does the sleep timer work?

Pick a length (15–90 minutes). When you start playback the timer arms. When it expires, playback stops cleanly. Selecting Off disables it. Changing the timer while playing extends or shortens the current run.

04

Audio behavior

Will it play with my phone on silent?

Yes — the app sets the iOS audio category to play in silent mode. Background playback is not enabled in this Expo Go build because that requires a custom native entitlement; if you lock your phone playback may suspend. The screen is kept awake while the app is open and playing.
