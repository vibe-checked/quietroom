Support

How can we help?

Have a question about mixing sounds, the sleep timer, or anything else? Check the FAQ below or reach out directly.

Do I need an internet connection?
No — every sound is generated on your device, and all playback works completely offline.
Why does my mix stop when I lock my phone?
Make sure "Keep playing when locked or backgrounded" is turned on in Settings (it's on by default). If it's off, playback pauses automatically when you leave the app.
How does the sleep timer work?
Tap the custom option under "Stop playing after" to open the Timer sheet, choose either a Duration (hours/minutes from now) or an End Time (an exact clock time), then tap Start. Playback fades out smoothly when it ends.
What are the Headphone Tones (binaural sounds)?
They're binaural beats — two very slightly different tones, one per ear. They only work correctly with headphones, since each ear needs to receive its own distinct tone.
Can I save a mix I like?
Yes — build a mix, then tap "+ Save current" under Saved Mixes to store it for one-tap access later.
How do I change the theme or language?
Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner to open Settings, where you can pick from 5 color themes and 6 languages.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy, simply explained

Last updated: August 9, 2026. This policy applies to the Quiet Room iOS application published by Mark Utility Labs.

🔒

Short version: Quiet Room runs entirely on your device. It generates every sound locally, doesn't require an account, and doesn't collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

1. What data the app handles

Quiet Room stores your saved mixes, edits to the Popular section, selected theme, language, and sleep-timer preferences directly on your device using standard iOS local storage. Nothing about how you use the app is ever sent anywhere.

2. How it's stored

All app data lives in local, on-device storage. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently — there's no cloud backup and no account to manage.

3. What is NOT collected

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No account or sign-in
  • No network requests of any kind — the app works fully offline

4. Permissions

Quiet Room does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos. It uses the standard iOS background-audio capability so your mix can keep playing when your screen is locked, and haptic feedback for taps — neither requires you to grant any permission.

5. Children's privacy

Quiet Room is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.
