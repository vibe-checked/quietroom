Features

What's in the build

The whole feature set, plainly listed — no upsells gating the basics.

◉

White noise

An even hiss across all frequencies — sharp, cuts conversation cleanly.

◍

Pink noise

Softer than white, lower energy at high frequencies — closer to leaves moving in wind.

◐

Brown noise

Deep, rumbling — like surf or a distant waterfall. Good for sleeping.

☂

Rain

Low-passed pink with scattered patter to approximate rainfall — steady, soothing.

☾

Sleep timer

15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes. Audio stops cleanly when the timer expires.

⊘

No network

The audio is synthesized in JavaScript on first launch and cached locally. After that, playback is fully offline.

How to play

From cold-start to good-at-it

A few short steps. The app gets out of your way after that.

01

Pick a sound

Tap one of the four chips. The big label updates and a short description appears.

02

Tap the big play button

First time on a given sound, the app synthesizes its WAV and caches it. After that, playback is instant.

03

Set a sleep timer (optional)

Tap a length. The timer arms when you start playback and stops audio cleanly when it expires.

04

Plays through silent mode

The app sets the iOS audio category so the sound plays even with your ringer switch off.
