Support

Frequently asked, honestly answered.

If your question isn't here, open an issue on GitHub. Public, traceable, and the same channel I use for everything else.

Where does the audio come from?

It's synthesized in JavaScript the first time you tap play. The app computes 10-second WAV files for each noise type using classic generators (Paul Kellett's pink filter, integrated Brownian motion for brown, low-passed pink + scattered impulses for rain), writes them to the cache, then loops via expo-av. After first launch, playback is fully offline.

Will it play with my phone on silent?

Yes — the app sets the iOS audio category to playback. Background playback when the phone is locked is not enabled in this Expo Go build because that requires a native entitlement; if you lock the phone, playback suspends.

How does the sleep timer work?

Pick a length (15–90 minutes). When you tap play with a timer set, it counts down; when it hits zero, playback stops cleanly. Off disables it.

Are any files shipped with the app?

No audio files are bundled. The WAVs are generated on-device and cached. That's why first play takes a second or two and why the entire app is small.

Does the app make any network requests?

No. No streaming, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no network calls of any kind. The settings (selected sound + timer length) live in AsyncStorage on this device.
