Support

Frequently asked, honestly answered.

If your question isn't here, open an issue on GitHub. Public, traceable, and the same channel I use for everything else.

Where do the sounds come from?

They're synthesized in JavaScript on first launch using classic noise generators — Paul Kellett's pink filter, integrated Brownian motion for brown, low-passed pink + impulses for rain. The 10-second WAV files are cached to disk and looped via expo-av.

Will it play with my phone on silent?

Yes — the app sets the iOS audio category to play in silent mode.

Will it keep playing if I lock the screen?

Background audio requires a native entitlement that isn't enabled in this Expo Go build. Locking the screen suspends playback. The screen is kept awake while the app is open and playing.

Why no nature recordings, ocean, forest?

Those need licensed audio files. Quiet Room ships zero audio assets on purpose — everything is synthesized. Recorded-sound packs are on the roadmap once we have a native build.
